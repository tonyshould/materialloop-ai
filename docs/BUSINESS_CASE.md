# MaterialLoop AI Business Case

## Customer problem

Manufacturers generate offcuts, turnings and other by-products that may retain useful material value. These materials often enter low-value recovery channels because each lot has incomplete evidence, inconsistent certificates and uncertain downstream requirements.

## Intended users

- Factory sustainability and operations teams that need a defensible route for industrial by-products.
- Recyclers and processors that need cleaner evidence before quoting or accepting a lot.
- Manufacturers that consume secondary material and need traceable compatibility information.

## Product value

MaterialLoop AI reduces the manual work required to organize evidence and compare conditional reuse routes. It also makes refusal visible: when the certificate is missing or the lot data conflicts, the system blocks valuation and matching instead of presenting a confident but unsupported answer.

## Commercial hypothesis

The first paid offer would be a limited factory pilot covering one material stream and a fixed number of monthly analyses. A validated pilot could expand into an annual site license with usage tiers. Network matching and multi-site reporting should follow only after factories demonstrate repeat use and data quality.

This is a business hypothesis for prototype evaluation. No customer contract, transaction volume or revenue result has been validated.

## Demo economics

The competition fixture compares traditional recovery of NTD 27,500 with a conditional second-life net value of NTD 35,500, a 29.1 percent uplift. The figures come from the versioned synthetic dataset `demo-price-tw-2026q3-v1` and do not represent a live offer.

## Scale path

1. Validate the aluminum workflow with a small number of factories.
2. Add verified processor and buyer requirement templates.
3. Add a second material only after the first workflow produces repeat use.
4. Introduce tenant controls, billing and production retention policies after paid demand exists.

## Impact measurement

The prototype reports material diverted, estimated avoided CO2e and virgin-material displacement using versioned synthetic factors. A production version would require geography, energy mix, processing route and lifecycle-boundary validation before reporting real environmental outcomes.
