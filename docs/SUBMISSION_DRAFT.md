**Brief description**

> MaterialLoop AI turns fragmented photos, certificates and process data into an auditable material-reuse decision. Gemini 2.5 Flash on Vertex AI extracts material facts, evidence gaps and candidate pathways. A deterministic evidence gate then controls the final state: ready for review, insufficient evidence or evidence conflict. The React and Express application runs on Google Cloud Run and emits structured Cloud Logging events. In the live prototype, three end-to-end cases demonstrate a reviewable recommendation, a refusal when evidence is missing and a block when evidence conflicts. The business path starts with one paid industrial pilot, measuring review time, evidence completeness and recovered material value before expanding to a B2B license.
